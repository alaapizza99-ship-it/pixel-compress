PixelCompress – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

PixelCompress ("the app") lets you compress, resize, crop, blur and convert photos on your phone. Your privacy matters, so this policy explains in plain words what the app does and does not do with your data.

Your photos stay on your device

All photo processing happens locally on your phone. The app does not upload your photos to any server, and we do not receive, view or store them.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app has no accounts or sign-in, and it does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.

Permissions

  • Photos: you choose the photos to process through Android's photo picker. The app only accesses the photos you select.
  • Saving to your gallery: when you tap Save, the processed photos are written to a "PixelCompress" album on your device.

Third parties

The app does not currently share data with any third party. If we add features such as advertising or paid subscriptions in a future version, we will update this policy before they are released and explain what data those services use.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at this page, with the date above.
